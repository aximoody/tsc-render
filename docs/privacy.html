Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. Applies to the "tsc-poster" application operated for the TheSecretCodex content channel.

This application ("the App") is used solely to publish short-form video content created by the operator to the operator's own TikTok account via the TikTok Content Posting API.

Information We Collect

The App accesses only the operator's own TikTok account through official TikTok OAuth authorization. It uses this access exclusively to upload videos that the operator has produced. The App does not collect, store, sell, or share personal data of any TikTok users, viewers, or third parties.

How We Use Access

Access is used only to (a) authenticate the operator's TikTok account and (b) upload the operator's own video files and captions. No data is used for advertising, profiling, or resale.

Data Storage

Authorization tokens are stored securely as encrypted secrets in the operator's own automation environment and are never shared with third parties.

Data Deletion

The operator may revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok settings. Upon revocation, all stored tokens become invalid and are deleted.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the operator through the TheSecretCodex channel.
